About Our Collective

The Artist Collective is a creative open space for students in Vienna. We are a community in the making. We make art, talk art, learn, share and discover together and encourage each other in our own artistic practices.

We are open to all artists and art lovers, from all background, experience, origin and gender. No discrimination of any kind will be tolerated.

We organise workshops, excursions, exhibition and collaborative projects.
